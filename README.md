## Hi!

I'm interested at the intersection of AI and Hardware generally speaking.

Current plans are incoming at Etched for research engineering on agents for hardware this winter and Optiver for quantitative research on the D1 team.

Some things I've worked on:

  exploring interfaces between AI and machines.

- **[CNC-RLVR](https://github.com/pqori/CNC-RLVR)** —
  benchmarking and reinforcement learning for CNC planning.
  Presenting this work at the PhysUnderstand workshop at NeurIPS 2026.

- **[GDPx](https://gdpx.semianalysis.com/domains/hardware-design)** —
  contributed AI evaluations for computer-aided manufacturing with
  **Beautiful Machines**, as part of **SemiAnalysis's GDPx benchmark**
